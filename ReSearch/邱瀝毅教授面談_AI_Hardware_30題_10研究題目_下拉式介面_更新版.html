必考題 1：IC 設計未來哪個方向是主流？

我認為未來主流會以 AI Hardware 為核心，整合低功耗設計、Memory-centric Computing、Chiplet、硬體安全與 AI-assisted EDA。若選一個最適合我投入的議題，我會選「低功耗、記憶體感知的 AI Accelerator」。

必考題 2：你想做哪個議題？

我目前最有興趣的是低功耗、記憶體感知的 AI Accelerator，尤其是 Transformer 或多模態模型在 Edge 裝置上的推論加速；但我願意配合實驗室計畫，先從 RTL、驗證或 baseline 重現開始。

我最推薦你背熟的 1 分鐘版本

老師您好，我目前最有興趣的方向是 AI Accelerator 與 Edge AI Hardware Architecture。我認為未來大型 AI 模型、多模態 AI 與生成式 AI 的發展，都需要更高效率、更低功耗的硬體支援，因此我希望未來能投入 AI 晶片設計相關研究。不過我也了解，要做 AI Hardware，必須先具備扎實的數位 IC 基礎，所以我希望先在實驗室學習 RTL 設計、數位電路、驗證與硬體架構分析，再逐步深入 AI Accelerator、Memory Architecture 等研究方向，並依照老師目前的研究計畫調整自己的研究題目。

研究能力發展流程圖

建議面談時不要只說「AI 晶片」，而要從 問題、瓶頸、方法、評估指標 四層表達：大型 AI 模型的瓶頸是資料搬移與記憶體頻寬；因此希望研究低功耗、記憶體感知的加速器；方法可包含資料流、mixed precision、CIM、NoC 或 KV cache；最後以 PPA、延遲、能耗與準確率驗證。

Good morning, Professor. I am currently studying Electrical Engineering at NCKU. I am particularly interested in digital IC design, VLSI/CAD, and AI hardware. I have worked on digital logic, a pipelined CPU, embedded systems, and AI-related projects. I hope to strengthen my RTL, verification, and microarchitecture skills before moving toward low-power AI accelerators, edge AI, and memory-aware computing.

我注意到老師的研究能把低功耗、記憶體、AI 加速器、多核心系統、VLSI CAD 與硬體安全串成完整脈絡。這和我希望培養的能力非常一致。我想學習從問題定義、文獻閱讀、架構設計、RTL 實作到量化評估的完整研究流程。

English Answer

Your research connects low-power design, memory, AI accelerators, multicore systems, VLSI CAD, and hardware security. This matches the kind of training I am looking for. I hope to learn the complete research process, from problem definition and literature review to architecture design, RTL implementation, and quantitative evaluation.

03你覺得 IC 設計未來哪個方向才是主流？Which IC design directions will become mainstream in the future?

中文回答

我認為未來主流不是單一領域，而是以 AI Hardware 為核心，整合低功耗設計、記憶體中心運算、Chiplet/異質整合、硬體安全與 AI-assisted EDA。若要選一個最適合老師實驗室、也適合我投入的方向，我會選「低功耗、記憶體感知的 AI Accelerator」，因為大型模型的核心瓶頸常在資料搬移、頻寬與能源效率，而不是只有運算單元數量。

English Answer

I believe the future mainstream will not be a single isolated field. It will center on AI hardware integrated with low-power design, memory-centric computing, chiplet-based heterogeneous integration, hardware security, and AI-assisted EDA. If I had to choose one direction that best fits your laboratory and my interests, I would choose low-power, memory-aware AI accelerators, because the major bottlenecks of large models are often data movement, bandwidth, and energy efficiency rather than only compute capacity.

04如果老師問你想做哪個議題，你要怎麼回答？Which research topic would you like to work on?

中文回答

我目前最有興趣的是「低功耗、記憶體感知的 AI Accelerator」，尤其是 Transformer 或多模態模型在 Edge 裝置上的推論加速。我想研究如何減少資料搬移、改善記憶體頻寬瓶頸，並在效能、功耗與面積之間取得平衡。不過我也知道實際題目需要配合實驗室目前的計畫，因此願意先從老師建議的 RTL、驗證或 baseline 重現開始。

English Answer

I am most interested in low-power, memory-aware AI accelerators, especially for Transformer or multimodal inference on edge devices. I would like to study how to reduce data movement, alleviate memory bandwidth bottlenecks, and balance performance, power, and area. I also understand that the actual topic should align with the lab’s ongoing projects, so I am willing to begin with RTL implementation, verification, or baseline reproduction under your guidance.

05為什麼選 AI Hardware，而不是只做 AI 軟體？Why AI hardware instead of only AI software?

中文回答

現代 AI 的瓶頸已不只是模型準確率，也包含延遲、能耗、記憶體容量與資料搬移。軟體最佳化只能改善一部分，真正的效率仍取決於資料路徑、記憶體階層、平行度與硬體排程。我希望把模型特性轉化成可實作的硬體架構。

English Answer

The bottlenecks of modern AI are no longer limited to model accuracy; latency, energy, memory capacity, and data movement are equally important. Software optimization helps, but efficiency is fundamentally determined by the datapath, memory hierarchy, parallelism, and hardware scheduling. I want to translate model characteristics into practical hardware architectures.

06你對老師研究方向的理解是什麼？What is your understanding of my research directions?

中文回答

我理解老師長期主軸是高能源效率智慧晶片，近年聚焦 SRAM/CIM、AI 加速器、多核心與 NoC、Transformer 映射、VLSI CAD 快速分析，以及 RISC-V 與 AI 系統安全。共同核心是減少資料搬移、提升可擴充性，並同時考慮 PPA 與安全性。

English Answer

My understanding is that your long-term theme is energy-efficient intelligent chips, with recent work focusing on SRAM/CIM, AI accelerators, multicore and NoC systems, Transformer mapping, fast VLSI CAD analysis, and RISC-V or AI system security. A common objective is to reduce data movement while improving scalability, PPA, and security.

07你目前具備哪些相關能力？What relevant skills do you currently have?

中文回答

我具備基本數位邏輯、Verilog/RTL、有限狀態機、Pipeline CPU、C/C++ 與 Python 能力，也接觸過嵌入式系統及 AI 模型。我希望進一步學習 SystemVerilog、驗證方法、FPGA 原型、效能分析與記憶體系統。

English Answer

I have foundational skills in digital logic, Verilog/RTL, finite-state machines, pipelined CPU design, C/C++, and Python, along with some experience in embedded systems and AI models. I hope to further develop SystemVerilog, verification, FPGA prototyping, performance analysis, and memory-system skills.

08你最需要補強什麼？What do you need to improve the most?

中文回答

我最需要補強大型 RTL 專案的模組化設計、SystemVerilog 驗證、時序與 PPA 分析，以及把論文規格轉成可重現實驗的能力。我會把這些列為加入實驗室後前六個月的主要學習目標。

English Answer

I need to improve modular design for larger RTL projects, SystemVerilog verification, timing and PPA analysis, and the ability to convert paper specifications into reproducible experiments. These would be my main learning goals during the first six months.

09你如何開始一個陌生研究題目？How would you start an unfamiliar research topic?

中文回答

我會先定義問題、工作負載與評估指標，再閱讀一篇 survey、數篇代表性論文與最新頂會論文，整理 baseline、瓶頸、假設與限制。接著先重現 baseline，建立可量測平台，再提出一個小而可驗證的改進。

English Answer

I would first define the problem, workload, and evaluation metrics. Then I would read a survey, representative papers, and recent top-conference work to summarize baselines, bottlenecks, assumptions, and limitations. I would reproduce a baseline first, build a measurable platform, and then propose a focused and testable improvement.

10你如何閱讀 IC 或架構論文？How would you read an IC or architecture paper?

中文回答

我會分成問題、方法、實作與實驗四層閱讀。問題層確認瓶頸是否真實；方法層理解資料流、記憶體與控制；實作層檢查位寬、頻率、製程、面積與工具；實驗層檢查 baseline、公平性、消融與限制。最後畫出架構圖並整理成一頁摘要。

English Answer

I read papers in four layers: problem, methodology, implementation, and experiments. I examine whether the bottleneck is real, how the dataflow and memory system work, what implementation assumptions are used, and whether the evaluation is fair. I then redraw the architecture and summarize the paper on one page.

11你如何證明一個新架構有效？How would you prove that a new architecture is effective?

中文回答

除了功能正確，我會比較 latency、throughput、power、energy、area、memory traffic 與 accuracy impact。也會做消融實驗、不同模型與不同工作負載測試，並把控制與記憶體成本納入。

English Answer

Beyond functional correctness, I would compare latency, throughput, power, energy, area, memory traffic, and accuracy impact. I would also conduct ablation studies and evaluate multiple models and workloads while including control and memory overhead.

12你認為 AI 加速器最大的瓶頸是什麼？What is the biggest bottleneck in AI accelerators?

中文回答

很多情況下不是 MAC 數量不足，而是權重與 activation 的資料搬移、片外記憶體頻寬、低利用率與不規則工作負載。對 Transformer 而言，attention、KV cache、長序列與不同算子之間的資料流也很重要。

English Answer

In many cases, the main bottleneck is not the number of MAC units but data movement for weights and activations, off-chip memory bandwidth, low utilization, and irregular workloads. For Transformers, attention, KV cache, long sequences, and dataflow across heterogeneous operators are also critical.

13為什麼記憶體系統重要？Why is the memory system important?

中文回答

資料搬移的能耗通常高於單次乘加運算。即使計算陣列很快，若資料供應不上，PE 仍會閒置。因此 SRAM 容量、banking、buffer reuse、NoC、DRAM 排程與壓縮都會直接影響能源效率。

English Answer

Data movement often consumes more energy than a multiply-accumulate operation. Even a fast compute array will be underutilized if data cannot be supplied efficiently. Therefore, SRAM capacity, banking, buffer reuse, NoC, DRAM scheduling, and compression directly affect energy efficiency.

14你對 Compute-in-Memory 的看法？What is your view on Compute-in-Memory?

中文回答

CIM 能把部分運算移到記憶體附近，降低資料搬移，但並非所有問題的答案。必須考慮 precision、ADC/DAC 成本、變異、可程式性、模型映射與系統整合。我對數位 SRAM-CIM 或 memory-centric accelerator 特別有興趣。

English Answer

CIM can reduce data movement by moving computation closer to memory, but it is not a universal solution. Precision, converter overhead, variation, programmability, model mapping, and system integration must be considered. I am particularly interested in digital SRAM-CIM or memory-centric accelerators.

15你會選 CNN 還是 Transformer 加速器？Would you choose a CNN or Transformer accelerator?

中文回答

如果是建立基礎，我會先從 CNN 或 GEMM accelerator 熟悉資料流與 buffer 設計；研究題目則希望進一步做 Transformer，因為它包含 GEMM、attention、softmax、normalization 與 KV cache 等更完整的系統挑戰。

English Answer

For foundational training, I would start with a CNN or GEMM accelerator to learn dataflow and buffering. For research, I would move toward Transformer acceleration because it includes GEMM, attention, softmax, normalization, and KV cache, providing richer system-level challenges.

16你為什麼想做 Edge AI？Why are you interested in edge AI?

中文回答

Edge AI 可以降低雲端延遲、保護隱私並提升即時性，但受限於功耗、記憶體與散熱。這使硬體架構的價值非常明確，也適合把低功耗、記憶體與 AI 工作負載整合成具體研究。

English Answer

Edge AI reduces cloud latency, improves privacy, and supports real-time operation, but it is constrained by power, memory, and thermal limits. This makes hardware architecture highly valuable and provides a concrete setting for combining low-power design, memory systems, and AI workloads.

17你對多模態 AI Hardware 的理解是什麼？What is your understanding of multimodal AI hardware?

中文回答

多模態系統需要處理文字、影像、語音或感測資料，算子型態、資料率與延遲需求不同。研究重點不只是增加運算單元，而是設計異質運算核心、共享記憶體、融合排程與動態資源配置。

English Answer

Multimodal systems process text, images, audio, or sensor data with different operators, data rates, and latency requirements. The challenge is not simply adding more compute units but designing heterogeneous cores, shared memory, fusion scheduling, and dynamic resource allocation.

18如何處理模型準確率與硬體效率的取捨？How would you balance model accuracy and hardware efficiency?

中文回答

我會把 accuracy、latency、energy 與 area 放在同一個 Pareto 分析中，透過 quantization、pruning、operator fusion 與 mixed precision 找到可接受的平衡，而不是只追求單一指標最佳。

English Answer

I would analyze accuracy, latency, energy, and area together using a Pareto framework. Techniques such as quantization, pruning, operator fusion, and mixed precision can be used to find a practical balance rather than optimizing only one metric.

19你如何看待 Chiplet 與異質整合？What is your view on chiplets and heterogeneous integration?

中文回答

Chiplet 能把不同製程、功能與記憶體模組拆開最佳化，再透過高頻寬互連整合。未來 AI 系統可能由 CPU、NPU、HBM 與安全模組共同組成，因此封裝、NoC、熱與一致性會成為重要研究問題。

English Answer

Chiplets allow different processes, functions, and memory modules to be optimized separately and integrated through high-bandwidth interconnects. Future AI systems may combine CPUs, NPUs, HBM, and security modules, making packaging, NoC, thermal management, and coherence important research topics.

20硬體安全和 AI 晶片有什麼關係？How is hardware security related to AI chips?

中文回答

AI 晶片可能面臨模型竊取、權重竄改、側信道、記憶體攻擊與惡意韌體。安全機制不能只加在軟體層，還需要 Root of Trust、Secure Boot、存取控制與低成本監測機制。

English Answer

AI chips may face model extraction, weight tampering, side-channel leakage, memory attacks, and malicious firmware. Security cannot be handled only in software; hardware roots of trust, secure boot, access control, and low-cost monitoring are also needed.

21你對 RISC-V 的看法？What is your view on RISC-V?

中文回答

RISC-V 的開放 ISA 適合學術研究與客製化加速器整合，可延伸指令、加入安全機制或設計 domain-specific coprocessor。但研究仍要證明客製化帶來的效益，並避免只停留在新增指令。

English Answer

RISC-V is an open ISA well suited for academic research and custom accelerator integration. It supports instruction extensions, security mechanisms, and domain-specific coprocessors. However, research should demonstrate measurable system benefits rather than merely adding instructions.

22你會如何做 RTL 驗證？How would you verify RTL?

中文回答

我會先建立規格與 assertion，再做模組級 testbench、corner cases、random test 與 coverage。整合後進行功能模擬、時序檢查、FPGA 驗證，必要時加入 formal verification。

English Answer

I would first define the specification and assertions, then build module-level testbenches, corner cases, random tests, and coverage. After integration, I would perform functional simulation, timing checks, FPGA validation, and formal verification when appropriate.

23如果實驗結果不好，你怎麼辦？What would you do if the experimental results are poor?

中文回答

我會先確認實作、量測與 baseline 是否正確，再分解瓶頸，檢查是不是記憶體、控制、利用率或參數造成。即使結果不如預期，也會保留負面結果，重新縮小假設，而不是只挑有利數據。

English Answer

I would first verify the implementation, measurements, and baseline. Then I would decompose the bottleneck and check memory behavior, control overhead, utilization, and parameters. Even if results are disappointing, I would preserve negative findings and refine the hypothesis rather than selecting only favorable data.

24你如何安排研究進度？How would you manage research progress?

中文回答

我會把題目拆成文獻、baseline、架構、RTL、驗證、實驗與寫作七個里程碑，每週提交可檢查的成果，例如摘要、程式、波形、表格或圖。遇到問題會提早回報，不讓風險累積到最後。

English Answer

I would divide the project into seven milestones: literature review, baseline, architecture, RTL, verification, experiments, and writing. Each week I would produce something reviewable, such as a summary, code, waveform, table, or figure. I would report risks early instead of allowing them to accumulate.

English Answer

Yes. I understand that research does not always begin with a cutting-edge topic. If I am assigned RTL modules, a simulator, a test platform, or paper reproduction, I will treat it as essential training for reliable research.

English Answer

I hope to complete a reproducible AI-accelerator baseline that runs on FPGA or RTL simulation with basic verification and performance analysis. Then I would propose one focused improvement in memory traffic, utilization, or power and produce a complete report.

27你如何與學長姐合作？How would you collaborate with senior students?

English Answer

I would first study existing documents and code, organize my questions, and then ask for guidance. During collaboration, I would clearly document interfaces, versions, and experimental settings, report progress on time, and ensure that my module can be integrated by others.
